    Associate in Arts and Sciences (DTA)

    About this demo

    The plan checks itself against the general Associate in Arts and Sciences DTA as printed in the 2025–26 Whatcom Community College catalog (pages 77–79). Course designations come from the 2026–27 catalog appendices; titles and credits from the 2026–27 degree requirement pages. It is a planning demo, not an official degree audit.

    What the demo leaves out.

    • 410 college-level courses (100–299) with titles and credits: 208 with DTA attributes from the catalog appendix, and 202 with none listed there, which the demo counts as unlisted electives. Bachelor's-level (300+) and pre-college (under 100) courses are left out.
    • Courses with a credit range, such as PHIL& 115 (3–5), go into a plan at 5 credits, or the most the range allows below that. Change the number on the course's card or in its details.
    • Not here yet: 261 courses the appendix lists but whose titles and credits are not on file, including PE (34), HIST (29), ENGL (23), MUSC (16), HUM (12), IDS (11), FILM (10), PSYC (10), FRCH (8), DANCE (7), DRMA (7), GEOG (7), PHYS (7), GEOL (6) and others.
    • Tick Completed on a course to record that the student finished it. The Degree audit tab shows what is done and what is only planned. Completed courses are not checked against a transcript.
    • No transcript, grades, placement or transfer credit. Placement and pre-college prerequisites (such as MATH 99) show as “Check”. Minimum grades are listed but not checked.
    • Not modeled: the six-credit HUM/IDS exception, independent-study caps, the PE activity cap, residency, GPA, S-grade and prior-learning limits, and when courses are offered or have seats.
    • When a course could count in more than one place, the demo tries each choice and keeps the one that meets the most requirements.
